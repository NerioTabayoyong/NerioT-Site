About

Hello! I’m Nerio Tabayoyong but most call me Jr. I’m a vis comm major from Daly City taking this course to further my own creative endeavors for the future! I love the process of creating. Whether it’s designing a graphical element, editing a long form video, or formulating a plan for a much larger project. Recently I found that my passions lie in video editing, since it’s the most satisfying for me to work on and combine all other forms of design into a large project.I’ve been creating for as long as I can remember and I hope I can show the world my skills!

My career goal is to become a freelance designer and video editor. However I need to get my name out there if I actually want people to pay me to work on projects. I think a website would be a great all-encompassing way for me to show off my portfolio, link all my socials, and also further show-off my design skills through the website itself. I think if I paid someone to make my website for me that entirely defeats the whole point of the site so coding it myself would be beneficial to me.
