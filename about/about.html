About Me


Hey, all! Nerio here!


Check out my YouTube channel!

Hello! I’m Nerio Tabayoyong but most call me Jr. I’m a vis comm major from Daly City taking this course to further my own creative endeavors for the future! I love the process of creating. Whether it’s designing a graphical element, editing a long form video, or formulating a plan for a much larger project. Recently I found that my passions lie in video editing, since it’s the most satisfying for me to work on and combine all other forms of design into a large project.I’ve been creating for as long as I can remember and I hope I can show the world my skills!


My career goal is to become a freelance designer and video editor. However I need to get my name out there if I actually want people to pay me to work on projects. I think a website would be a great all-encompassing way for me to show off my portfolio, link all my socials, and also further show-off my design skills through the website itself. I think if I paid someone to make my website for me that entirely defeats the whole point of the site so coding it myself would be beneficial to me.


This process was relatively straightforward. Maybe it’s because I’ve submitted three of these projects already but I think I’m surely yet slowly getting the hang of Visual Studio Code. I’m surprised too since, by the time of the very first project, when the professor was giving his lesson I was already semi-familiar with concepts such as tags and the like that the only real issue was forgetting about the slash key to close certain tags.


Nothing about iframe confused me. The example code was very straightforward and easy to understand. The only real thing that I needed to change was the dimensions of the video. I recorded my introduction video vertically on my phone, so it was as simple as just changing the dimensions around. I suppose another hiccup that I encountered was that, initially, the video didn’t show up on my test site. But then I found out that the web browser I use blocks out “suspicious” links. And once I figured that out and turned it off the video showed up normally.


I choose to place my hyperlink to my personal YouTube channel. It’s practically the closest thing I have to a portfolio as it has videos that I personally created. As well as a little portfolio playlist of other projects that I worked on and am personally proud of.
